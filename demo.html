This is a fully self-contained replay of the 2022 Qatar World Cup, run through the sweepstake's scoring and subsumptive-league rules. No backend — everything you see is computed in your browser from the final 2022 results. Colours here are by tier, not by player.

  • Players draft for themselves, so round 1 (Tier 1) is where the top seeds go and the weakest teams fall to the last tier. Here we tiered the 30 teams by FIFA ranking to simulate that draft.
  • Leagues are subsumptive. League L contains every team whose tier ≥ L. So League 1 is the grand league with all 30 teams; each higher league strips out the top tier of seeds, down to League 5 (only the six lowest seeds).
  • Underdogs sweep. A low-seeded team that goes deep appears in many leagues at once, so it can win several prize pots. In 2022, Morocco (Tier 4) tops Leagues 3 and 4.
  • Per-player league: each player's score is the sum of all the teams they drafted — the table just below.
  • Points: Win 3 · Draw 1 · ET win 2 · Penalty-shootout win 4 (the 90-min draw scores 1, plus 3). Each knockout round reached adds a +2 bonus.
